# OIB.Guide — Voice

## Tone
Hyper-local Brunswick County NC. Beach-real, not tourism-board. Specific over generic. Humor allowed when it's earned. Confident, not salesy.

## What to sound like

- "Made in Ocean Isle Beach for people who actually live the beach life."
- "If you've walked Causeway Drive in flip-flops, this is your shirt."
- "Built for the Calabash crowd."
- "From the porch of a Sunset Beach cottage."
- References to real local spots: OIB Pier, the boardwalk fish shack, Causeway Drive, Holden Beach bridge, Calabash seafood row, Shallotte River.

## What NOT to sound like

**Banned vocabulary** (these are tourism-board cliché — never use):
- "tropical paradise"
- "luxury escape"
- "exotic getaway"
- "your slice of heaven"
- "island vibes" (we're not an island, we're a coastal town on the mainland)
- "aloha" (wrong coast)
- "palm trees swaying" (we have live oaks and yaupon, not palms)
- "endless summer" (we have storm season and shoulder seasons — be real)
- Generic Etsy keyword stuffing without local anchor

## Brand notes

- Audience splits two ways: locals (year-round residents) and returning summer visitors (people with a regular beach week, not first-timers). Both want SPECIFIC not generic.
- OIB.Guide is the directory + brand for Ocean Isle Beach but covers Brunswick County broadly: Calabash, Shallotte, Sunset Beach, Holden Beach, Bolivia, Supply.
- Audience knows the difference between OIB and "Outer Banks" — never conflate. NC coast ≠ Outer Banks.
- Tourist season is roughly Memorial Day → Labor Day; shoulder season Apr-May + Sep-Oct; quiet Nov-Mar. Listings can lean seasonal but should hold up year-round.
- Local pride > generic beach pride. "OIB Local" > "Beach Lover."
